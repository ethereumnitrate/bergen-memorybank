# Bergen Memory Bank - Decisions, Reflections, and Reusable Practices

Keep one shared Decisions, Reflections, and Reusable Practices document for durable decisions, reflections, and reusable practices that can inform more than one course.

## Safe-data boundary

Use faculty decisions and de-identified teaching observations only. Do not enter student records, raw submissions, names, emails, IDs, individual grades, student-specific feedback, accommodations, health details, identifiable quotations, or identifying combinations. Keep protected data in Canvas.

## Ownership and use

**Primary home:** Durable decisions, reflections, and reusable practices that apply across courses.

Each fact has one primary home. Stable faculty preferences belong in the Faculty Profile, durable course-specific facts belong in that course's Course Memory, and temporary work belongs in that course's Active Workbench.

Before recording, confirm that the entry is reusable across courses. If it applies to only one course, use that course's Course Memory or Active Workbench instead.

## Recording approved updates

`bergen:record` produces a proposal only. After faculty approval, it must name exactly one primary target document and supply copy-ready text. Paste the approved text manually into this Google Doc. The Gem does not save, synchronize, modify, or retain documents automatically.
